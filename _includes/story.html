OUR STORY

Proudly Canadian-owned.

Built for real homes and commercial needs in Ontario.

Custom-built for real homes

We didn’t start as a company — we started with a real need at home.

My wife is a piano musician and piano teacher, and her studio was filled with unwanted echo and distracting noise. We wanted to create a space that felt calmer, sounded better, and supported the quality of every lesson and performance. That’s where our acoustic panel journey began.

As we refined our designs, we realized the same attention to sound control, protection, and clean aesthetics could also transform golf simulator spaces. Many home setups lacked proper impact protection, created too much noise, or simply didn’t feel finished.

Today, Canada Panel Pro is a proudly Canadian-owned business based in Ancaster, Ontario.

We proudly serve Hamilton, Burlington, Toronto, GTA area, Barrie, Niagara area, Kitchner area, London, and Windsor.
